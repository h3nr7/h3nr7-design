Why JPEG fails as elevation

  • 8 bits gives 256 height levels: terrain steps.
  • Lossy compression shows up as bumps.
  • No absolute scale between tiles.

GeoTIFF stores 16- or 32-bit heights with georeferencing, so every vertex has a real coordinate.
